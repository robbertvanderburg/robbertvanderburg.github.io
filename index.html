How extreme weather affects power grids

How likely is it that several parts of a power grid fail during the same storm? Wind, floods and heatwaves affect whole regions, exposing nearby components to similar stresses.

I compare Ising models, Bayesian hierarchical models, autologistic regression and independent failure models to see how assumptions about shared weather exposure change estimates of network reliability.

This work is part of the Vidi project Power Network Optimization in the Age of Climate Extremes. My supervisors are Alessandro Zocca and Frank van der Meulen.
