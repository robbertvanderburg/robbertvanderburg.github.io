# Robbert van der Burg

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Mathematicians, network scientists and prospective collaborators come to understand Robbert’s work and read his papers. Students, recruiters and seminar organizers also need his education, talks, teaching and university contact details.

## Product Purpose

A personal research website for a doctoral researcher working on probability, spatial dependence and the reliability of infrastructure networks at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. Readers should reach a paper, slide deck or contact address directly.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Keep the four existing Jekyll routes, GitHub Pages deployment, search-engine metadata, author exclusions and automated Semantic Scholar bibliography. The newest indexed preprint takes priority immediately after the homepage introduction. News is editorially maintained. Paper status, author order and identifiers follow the source record.

## Brand Commitments

Use the existing portrait, wine-red identity and three established type families. Use first-person prose for Robbert’s research and plainly distinguish degree programs, project roles and preprints.

## Evidence on Hand

The official VU researcher profile, repository CV, seminar slides, NWO project record and linked arXiv abstracts support the academic content. The portrait and presentation files belong to the existing site.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Aim for WCAG 2.2 AA in both themes. Support keyboard navigation, reduced motion, text enlargement and reading the bibliography without JavaScript.
